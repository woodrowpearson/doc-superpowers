# Orchard grafting guide

This guide explains how the orchard team grafts young trees in late winter. It covers the stock, the scion and the tying, and it names the codes used in the planting plan.

## Stock and scion

### RS2. Rootstock for dry slopes

Choose RS2 for every block that loses its topsoil in summer, since its roots reach deeper water than the standard stock does and the trees seldom need irrigation after the second year.

The scion is the cutting that carries the variety you want to grow, and it must come from a tree that fruited well in the last two seasons, was free of canker, and was cut while the wood was still dormant and cold.

### Tying and sealing

Wrap the union with grafting tape from the bottom upward, overlapping each turn by half its width, and seal the cut tip of the scion with wax so that the bud cannot dry out before the sap rises.

The plum variety (FT5) is grafted first because it wakes earliest. Later in the guide FT5 is mentioned again, and it needs no further gloss.

Blocks marked PQ88 in the old plan were never replanted.

## Key

- **MG3** the medium-vigour stock used on the flat blocks.
